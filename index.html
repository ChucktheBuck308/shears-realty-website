The honest number

Right now, buying near campus is a rent-avoidance play — not an appreciation play.

You will hear a lot about long-run appreciation in College Station. Over a long enough window that story is real. It is also not what the market is doing this year — values here have been essentially flat.

So we do not lead with it. The case for buying a place for your student is the rent you stop paying and what roommates cover while they live there. That case is often strong. Sometimes it is not, and we will say so before you write a cheque, not after.
