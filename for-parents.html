What is deliberately not in this table: appreciation. Values in College Station have been roughly flat over the past year. Over decades the trend has been upward, and you will see that long-run figure quoted often — but a four-year hold is not a decades-long hold, and we are not going to build your decision on a number the current market is not producing. If the property appreciates, treat it as upside you did not pay for. If the deal only works with appreciation, it does not work.

Straight answers

When we tell families not to buy.

Your student has under three years left

Closing costs on both ends eat a short hold. Under three years, renting usually wins outright.

You would be stretched by two vacant months

Student rentals turn on a calendar, not on demand. If a summer gap would hurt, this is not the right purchase.

Nobody wants to be a landlord

Someone has to handle a broken water heater in July. If that is not you and management fees kill the margin, the maths has already answered you.

The building is mostly investor-owned already

Heavy investor concentration can affect financing and resale. We check this before you fall in love with a unit, not after.
